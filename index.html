Invent a Beepy!

Hi, I'm Poofle! In Beepies you scan the barcode on real stuff around your house, and a little creature like me pops out. What the product is decides what kind of Beepy you get. Help us dream up new ones!

The 12 elements

  • Nature: plants, grains, wood (cereal)
  • Earth: rocks, salt, clay (table salt)
  • Fire: spicy and hot (hot sauce)
  • Water: water and washing (bottled water)
  • Ice: cold and frozen (ice cream)
  • Spark: electricity (AA batteries)
  • Metal: shiny metal things (foil)
  • Sweet: candy and sugar (gummy candy)
  • Beast: gentle animals (eggs)
  • Chem: factory-made stuff (school glue)
  • Air: light and fluffy (cotton balls, that's me!)
  • Glitch: a barcode that isn't anything

A few rules

  • Cute or cool, never scary or gross. Little kids play this.
  • It comes from a real product with a barcode.
  • Pick a product a kid can safely hold (no bleach, cleaners, medicine or lighters), and no meat. You can still scan that stuff in the game, it just won't be any Beepy's home.
  • Got a doodle? Text it to whoever sent you this page!
